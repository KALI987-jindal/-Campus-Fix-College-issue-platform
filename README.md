# 🏫 CampusFix — Multi-Faculty College Issue Platform

> A specialized college issue and grievance resolution platform with **Academic Department Scoping** (Engineering, Law, Dental, Management, etc.), **Teacher/Faculty workflows**, and **Campus-Wide Shared Infrastructure**.

---

## 📌 Core Architecture

### 1. Dual-Tier Scoping (No Department Clutter)
- **Department-Specific Scope:** Engineering lab issues stay within the Engineering department. Dental clinic issues stay within Dental. Law moot court issues stay within Law. Students and teachers from other departments are **not disturbed**.
- **Campus-Wide Scope:** Shared university facilities (Central Library, Canteens, Campus Buses, Sports Grounds, Central Hostels) are open to all students and teachers university-wide for reporting and upvoting.

### 2. User Roles
- 👨‍🎓 **Students:** Report issues in their department or shared campus areas, upvote common issues.
- 👩‍🏫 **Teachers / Faculty:** Can raise issues with an urgent **"Lecture-Critical" (15-min SLA)** tag during classes, verify lab repairs, and add faculty remarks.
- 👨‍💼 **Department HOD / Lab In-Charge:** Oversee department-level tickets, assign internal lab staff, or escalate to university maintenance.
- 🛠️ **Central Maintenance Technicians:** Electricians, plumbers, IT engineers, mechanics who resolve tickets and upload before/after photos.
- 🏛️ **University Super Admin / Dean:** Executive oversight across all departments, department comparison league tables, and statutory UGC/NAAC report exports.
